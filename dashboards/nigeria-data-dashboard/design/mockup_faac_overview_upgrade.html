ElementWhat changesBuilt asTrade-off
Freshness stripReplaces the refresh stamp. Monthly cadence, grace 45 days, warn 15 days: a month is due 45 days after it ends (September by 14 Nov). Up to 15 days late is amber, later is red. Use the buttons at the top to see each state.HTML measureAgreed.
Insight bannerNew, at the bottom of the page as the closing takeaway. Two rule-based sentences: net vs previous period, deductions share, and the top state. It follows the Period and Currency switches.HTML measureTakes about 60 px of height, so the charts get a little shorter.
Flip cardsThe four KPI groups become flip cards. Front: value, coloured change (▲▼■, same rule as today) and a 6-month SVG sparkline. Back: top 3 revenue streams for Gross and top 3 deduction lines for Deductions (amount and share, ranked live from the six lines each); a "What it means" sentence for Net and Exchange rate. A weaker naira shows red.HTML + SVGHover doesn't exist on phones, so the front must stand on its own. The existing tooltip moves to the native visuals only.
Line chartData labels on every 3rd month and the latest month (gross above, net below). Hover tooltip card: gross and net for the month, each with % change vs the previous month.Native + report-page tooltipLabelling all 24 months on 2 lines would overlap.
Revenue mixNo change.NativeKeeps tooltips and cross-filtering.
Rotating shelfReplaces the top-5 states bar chart. The leader sits in the centre with its net received as the big number and its share of the national net underneath. Large rank badge; the month sits in the subtitle. Rotates every 3 s and pauses on hover.HTML measureAgreed: no State Detail tooltip and no click-to-filter here.
